Each victory makes Forger stronger — permanent HP and SP bonuses carry forward. Conserve resources early. The Monster Mage shows no mercy.

Combat System

How to Play

BattleForge is a one-on-one tactical duel. Each round, you and the enemy simultaneously choose actions — attack for reliable damage, defend to absorb the next blow, spend Skill Points on a devastating special, or heal to recover HP.

⚔️
ATTACK

A direct strike. Free to use, always reliable. Your bread-and-butter action every round.

15 – 25 DMG · NO COST
🛡️
DEFEND

Brace for impact. Halves incoming damage and passively restores a small amount of HP and SP.

½ DAMAGE · +5 HP +5 SP
✨
SPECIAL

Channel raw power into one devastating blow. Use it early — an unspent SP pool is wasted potential.

30 – 45 DMG · COSTS 10 SP
💚
HEAL

Channel restorative energy to recover a significant chunk of HP. Essential in long fights.

+30 – 45 HP · COSTS 15 SP
Pro Tips
🎯Defend when below 30 HP — the AI hits harder when you're low.
⚡Use Special early and often. SP left unspent is wasted damage.
🧠Enemies defend too. Watch the log and attack right after they defend twice.
❤️Save 15 SP for Heal before the Boss fight. You'll need the recovery.
💀When the Monster Mage goes below 25% HP it enrages — finish it fast.
🏆Win early levels efficiently. Bonus HP and SP carry into harder fights.
